The process

Brief to post,
without the fog.

  1. 01

    Track fit

    You send the official sound, release context, timing, and campaign goal. We decide whether the account and format genuinely fit.

  2. 02

    Concept lock

    We align on creative lanes and prohibited claims before production. The story remains native to the account.

  3. 03

    Production

    We create original AI-assisted scenes, human-directed edits, captions, dialogue, and platform-ready variations.

  4. 04

    Publish + report

    Approved posts go live in the agreed window. We return a concise campaign recap using available first-party analytics.

Good to know

Do you guarantee views or streams?+

No. We guarantee only the deliverables written into the campaign scope. Platform performance is never guaranteed.

Can you use our official TikTok sound?+

Yes, provided the sound is available to the account and the campaign details are agreed before production.

Are sponsored posts disclosed?+

Yes. Paid partnerships are disclosed according to platform requirements and the agreed campaign terms.

Does a campaign include usage rights?+

Account posting and paid usage are separate rights. Any reposting, whitelisting, or advertising usage is scoped in writing.
